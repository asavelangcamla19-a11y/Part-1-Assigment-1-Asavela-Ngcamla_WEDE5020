Our Services & Programmes

Explore how the Eco System Marine Conservation Trust serves the Gqeberha community and protects our marine life.

1. Coastal & Beach Cleanups

We organize weekly cleanup drives across major Gqeberha beaches to prevent plastic from entering Algoa Bay.

  • When: Every Saturday, 08:00 – 10:30
  • Where: Rotating locations (Kings Beach, Summerstrand, Pollock Beach)
  • Provided: Gloves, collection bags, and safety equipment

2. Wildlife Rehabilitation Support

Working with local experts to rescue and care for endangered African Penguins and marine fauna in distress.
